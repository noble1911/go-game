How to play
  1. Black and White take turns placing a stone on an empty intersection. Black goes first.
  2. A group of stones is captured when the other colour fills its last liberty (adjacent empty point).
  3. You can't play where your stone would have no liberties, unless it captures.
  4. Ko: you can't recreate an earlier position, so a single-stone recapture must wait a move.
  5. When both players pass in a row, mark the dead stones and count. Your score is your stones plus the empty points only you surround; White gets komi for moving second.
